The Concept

PulseGuard: NeoNet is a sci-fi cyberpunk universe centered around an elite task force designated to defend the core digital infrastructure of a sprawling mega-city. When the digital ecosystem ("NeoNet") is threatened by corruptive viruses, rogue AI entities, and structural glitches, these operatives materialize digital manifestations of their lifeforce weapon systems to clean up the code and protect reality.

Each agent is synchronized to a unique spectral frequency, augmenting their physical gear, combat suits, and weapons with specialized energy pulses that react directly against digital corruption.

The Lore

In the year 2088, humanity fully integrated its consciousness into the NeoNet. True peace was short-lived. A sudden catastrophic anomaly known as the "Pulse-Fracture" split the network into volatile elemental nodes, birthing aggressive security breaches that threaten to tear down civilization from the inside out.

To combat this existential threat, the PulseGuard initiative was born. Five elite operatives were selected, each anchoring one core fundamental node of the NeoNet framework to maintain universal balance.
